A training kit for launching Christian discipleship groups among your staff and volunteers. It starts with the pitfalls that sink most groups, then gives you a six-session training plan, a weekly group rhythm and a covenant your early adopters can use from week one.

The model in one line

Small groups of 8 to 10 adults who meet weekly around Scripture, prayer and honest accountability, with a plan to multiply from day one.

Groups of 8 to 10 are small enough that everyone can speak each week and large enough that a couple of absences never stall the meeting. For the accountability portion, break into pairs or threes so the depth of a very small group is not lost inside the bigger one.

What is in this kit

  1. Pitfalls - 12 ways discipleship groups go wrong, each with a guardrail.
  2. Training - a six-session plan for your early adopters, with Scripture, practice and homework.
  3. Toolkit - weekly meeting rhythm, accountability questions, a group covenant, case studies and a facilitator checklist.
  4. Sources - where the research came from.

Assumptions behind this kit

  • Broadly evangelical, Bible-centered, and not tied to one denomination. Adjust wording to your tradition.
  • Groups are 8 to 10 adults. The sources behind some pitfalls studied very small groups of 3 or 4, so watch how depth holds at this size and lean on the pair-and-three breakouts.
  • Early adopters are 6 to 12 staff and volunteers who will each facilitate or co-facilitate a group afterward.
  • Training meets in person, weekly or every other week.
  • Groups are separate from anyone's job. Joining is voluntary.
