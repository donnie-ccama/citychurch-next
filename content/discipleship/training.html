Six sessions for early adopters

Discipleship group starter kit

OverviewPitfallsTrainingToolkitSources

Each session runs about 90 minutes: 15 minutes of welcome and prayer, 40 minutes of teaching and discussion, 25 minutes of practice, and 10 minutes to set homework and pray. The goal is for every early adopter to have been in a group, not just heard about one.

Session 1: Why we make disciples

ScriptureMatthew 28:18-20 · 2 Timothy 2:2 · Mark 3:14
GoalShare the vision and why it fits who you are.

Cover

  • Jesus called the twelve first to be with him, then to be sent. Discipleship is relationship before it is program.
  • Paul to Timothy: four generations in one verse. We train people who will train others.
  • Our tie-in: we feed hungry children because bread matters. We disciple our staff and volunteers because people do not live by bread alone (Deuteronomy 8:3, Matthew 4:4). Jesus is the bread of life (John 6:35).

Practice. Each person shares who has discipled them, formally or not, and what that person did.

Homework. Read Mark 1-3. Write your faith story in a form you could tell in 3 minutes.

Session 2: What a disciple is

ScriptureLuke 9:23 · John 15:1-11 · Acts 2:42
GoalAgree on a clear win the whole ministry can name.

Cover

  • Five marks of a growing disciple: abides in the Word, prays, lives in honest community, obeys what Jesus says, and joins his mission, including through the work we do for kids.
  • Growth is the fruit of abiding in Christ, not self-improvement.
  • Why Scripture memory matters, and how to start small.

Practice. Tell your 3-minute faith stories in pairs. Give each other one encouragement.

Homework. Start the daily reading plan. Memorize John 15:5.

Session 3: How a group works

ScriptureHebrews 10:24-25 · Acts 2:42-47
GoalExperience a real group meeting from start to finish.

Cover

  • Walk through the weekly rhythm in the Toolkit.
  • The trainer leads a full live meeting with the cohort as members.
  • Debrief: what helped, what felt awkward, what you would change.

Practice. Split into threes and practice the Scripture section using this week's reading.

Homework. Continue reading. Write down one step of obedience and try it this week.

Session 4: Pitfalls and guardrails

ScriptureGalatians 6:1-2 · Proverbs 27:17
GoalSee the common failure points before your groups hit them.

Cover

  • Walk through the 12 pitfalls on the Pitfalls page.
  • Which two worry you most for our ministry? Why?
  • Work through the case studies in the Toolkit.

Practice. Role-play two case studies. Rotate who plays the facilitator.

Homework. Read the covenant draft and mark anything you would change.

Session 5: Accountability, confidentiality and care

ScriptureJames 5:16 · Proverbs 27:6 · 1 Thessalonians 2:8
GoalBuild trust that is safe and honest.

Cover

  • Finalize the group covenant together.
  • Confidentiality and its limits: anything suggesting harm to self or others, or abuse of a child, is not kept confidential. Follow your organization's child protection policy and state reporting law.
  • When to refer someone to a pastor or counselor.
  • The supervisor rule, and why participation stays voluntary.

Practice. Practice the accountability questions in threes. The facilitator answers first.

Homework. Pray over your list of people you might invite into a group.

Session 6: Sent to multiply

Scripture2 Timothy 2:2 · John 20:21
GoalLaunch the first groups.

Cover

  • Form the first groups: 8 to 10 adults, no supervisor with a direct report.
  • Set a start date, meeting time and covenant end date for each group.
  • Name who each person will pray about inviting when their group multiplies.

Practice. Lay hands on and pray over each new facilitator in a short commissioning.

Homework. Hold your first group meeting within two weeks.
