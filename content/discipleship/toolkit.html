Accountability questions

Pick 3 to 5 as a group and use the same ones for a season.

  1. How has your time in God's Word been this week?
  2. What step of obedience did you name last week, and how did it go?
  3. Where have you seen God at work, including in our work with kids?
  4. Is there anything you need to confess or bring into the light?
  5. Who are you praying for who does not yet know Jesus?
  6. How are your closest relationships: family, friends, coworkers?
  7. How are you resting and caring for yourself?
  8. Have you been honest in your answers today?

Group covenant

Read it together and have each member sign. Fill in the blanks as a group.

  • We will meet every ____ at ____ from ____ until ____.
  • We will come prepared: reading done, memory verse practiced.
  • We will pray for each other by name during the week.
  • We will be honest, and we will let each other grow in honesty at our own pace.
  • What is shared here stays here, except anything suggesting harm to self or others or abuse of a child, which we will bring to the right people.
  • This group has nothing to do with anyone's job or volunteer role. No one is evaluated on it.
  • Anyone may step out at any time without penalty.
  • Before our end date, each of us will pray about who we could invite into a new group.

Case studies for Session 4

  1. One member talks for 20 minutes during every check-in. How do you redirect with kindness?
  2. Your group has skipped memory verses for three weeks. Does it matter? What do you do?
  3. A member wants to swap the reading plan for a popular Christian book everyone is talking about.
  4. A staff member's supervisor asks to join their group.
  5. During prayer, a member shares something that suggests a child may be at risk.
  6. Six months in, the group loves meeting and does not want to multiply.

Facilitator checklist

  • Pray for each member by name before the meeting.
  • Do the reading yourself first.
  • Watch the clock so prayer never gets squeezed.
  • Go first in honesty.
  • Follow up with anyone who missed.
  • Check in monthly with the ministry lead.
  • Recruit a co-facilitator from inside the group by month two, and hand them facilitation at least once a month.
Before launch. Have someone review the confidentiality wording against your organization's child protection policy and your state's reporting rules, since your staff and volunteers work with children.
